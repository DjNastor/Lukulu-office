Independent record label · South Africa

African roots.
Electronic pulse.

Lukulu Recordings is an independent South African record label focused on Afro House, Afro-Tech and 3-Step. The label's published profile describes its founding in 2012 in Ladysmith and its direction by producer DJ Nastor.

Who we are

Our sound

Music driven by rhythm, storytelling and South African electronic culture, designed to connect local talent and international listeners.

Creative direction

DJ Nastor leads the label's creative direction. The service agreement identifies Nhlakanipho Ntobeko Dlamini as its representative; this is not a substitute for official director/shareholder records.

What the label does

Music releases

Catalogue management, metadata, release preparation, artwork, audio and distributor delivery workflows.

Rights & publishing

Split sheets, registration preparation, usage reporting and royalty reconciliation.

Artist services

Smart EPKs, DJ bookings, promotion, radio pitching and social campaign reviews.

These are the label's operating areas and V42 product scope. Not every automated function is currently live.
